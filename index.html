POLI 110: Investigating Politics

An Introduction to Scientific Political Analysis

Tutorial materials prepared by Aodi Cheng, Graduate Teaching Assistant
Department of Political Science, University of British Columbia · 2024–25

This page collects the tutorial slides, lecture notes, and practice exercises I wrote as a teaching assistant for POLI 110. The course introduces the logic of empirical inquiry in political science: what makes a claim scientific, how concepts become measurable variables, where measurement and sampling error come from, and how researchers reason about causation.

Everything here is my own writing, released for students and instructors to reuse. Course readings, examination materials, and any records identifying students are deliberately not included.
